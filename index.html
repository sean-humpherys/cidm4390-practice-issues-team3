What is product backlog grooming?

Product Backlog grooming, or refinement, is the ongoing collaboration where the team reviews, estimates, and clarifies backlog items. It ensures upcoming user stories meet the Definition of Ready prior to Sprint Planning.

What is Daily Scrum, who participates, and what is the objective? What is not Daily Scrum?

The Daily Scrum is a timeboxed 15-minute sync for Developers to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt their daily plan. It is a focused peer planning session rather than an administrative status report.

What is a Sprint Review activity?

Held at the end of the Sprint, the Sprint Review is an interactive session where the Scrum team inspects the working increment alongside stakeholders. The team gathers direct feedback to collaboratively adapt the Product Backlog.

Read more…

What is a Sprint Retrospective activity?

The Sprint Retrospective provides dedicated time for the Scrum team to inspect how the last Sprint went regarding individuals, interactions, processes, and tools. The team identifies actionable improvements to implement in the next Sprint.

Read more…

What is the difference between a Sprint backlog and a Product Backlog?

While the Product Backlog is an evolving inventory of everything needed in the product, the Sprint Backlog is the focused subset chosen for the active Sprint. Understanding this boundary keeps work aligned with sprint goals.

What is Sprint execution?

Sprint execution encompasses the daily development, testing, and collaboration Developers perform to turn backlog items into a done increment. It focuses on flow, continuous communication, and removing blockers rapidly.

Who is Kenneth S. Rubin? What does he know about Scrum?

Kenneth S. Rubin is an Agile thought leader, trainer, and author of Essential Scrum, one of the most widely referenced guides in the industry. He created the Visual AGILExicon® to standardize how teams communicate agile concepts visually.

What are the responsibilities of a DevOps professional?

A DevOps professional bridges development and operations to automate testing, build pipelines, and manage cloud environments. Their work enables the Scrum team to deploy shippable software smoothly at the end of each Sprint.
